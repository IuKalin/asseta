## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-trust, institutional, yet deeply personal continuity and asset-governance environment. It directly serves founders, business owners, and heads of household who manage complex legal, financial, and digital estates.

The visual style blends **Corporate Modern** precision with **Refined Editorial Minimalism**. The aesthetic must evoke unflinching security, total operational preparedness, and calm authority. Interfaces avoid frivolous decoration or startup playfulness; every element communicates permanence, auditability, and absolute discretion.

Key attributes:
- **Architectural Clarity:** Strict structural alignments, deliberate division lines, and razor-sharp typographic hierarchy.
- **Dignified Restraint:** Restrained micro-interactions and intentional pacing that convey weight, fiduciary responsibility, and confidence.
- **Critical Legibility:** High-contrast data presentation engineered for swift comprehension during high-stress continuity transitions and succession handoffs.

## Colors

The palette is built on deep maritime slates, fortified emerald-teals, and focused amber warnings—balanced over clinical, crisp surfaces.

### Primary Foundation (Midnight Slate)
- `primary` (`#0B132B`): Primary structural base, foundational typography, high-impact buttons, and top-tier navigational structures.
- `primary-container` (`#1C2541`): Elevated dark panels, security vaults, active selection states in navigation sidebars.
- `primary-muted` (`#1E293B`): Deep neutral slate for secondary headings, heavy icons, and data table headers.

### Secondary Accent (Preparedness Emerald & Teal)
- `secondary` (`#0D9488`): Primary confirmation, verified continuity status indicators, active ledger signals.
- `secondary-light` (`#10B981`): Dynamic metrics, positive operational states, live sync pulses.
- `secondary-deep` (`#059669`): Deep emerald for high-contrast text badges, secure status pills, and interactive active rings.
- `secondary-subtle` (`#F0FDFA`): Background wash for verified items, active escrow banners, and success toasts.

### Tertiary Accent (Urgency Amber & Gold)
- `tertiary` (`#D97706`): Action-required markers, stale protocol warnings, critical emergency contact alerts.
- `tertiary-vibrant` (`#F59E0B`): Time-sensitive badges, signature-pending states, transition trigger states.
- `tertiary-subtle` (`#FFFBEB`): Background wash for warnings, unverified asset flags, and re-authentication prompts.

### Neutral & Surface Architecture
- `surface` (`#FFFFFF`): Primary background for operational cards, data fields, dialogs, and main canvases.
- `surface-dim` (`#F8FAFC`): Application page canvas, inactive list rows, embedded sub-panels.
- `surface-border` (`#E2E8F0`): Structural separator lines, perimeter card strokes, crisp data row dividers.
- `surface-border-subtle` (`#F1F5F9`): Secondary nested boundaries and subtle matrix dividers.
- `text-primary` (`#0F172A`): Master body text, values, names, and principal data metrics.
- `text-secondary` (`#475569`): Labels, field titles, context descriptions, audit timestamps.
- `text-tertiary` (`#94A3B8`): Placeholder values, deactivated features, metadata keys.

## Typography

Typography pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for commanding, trustworthy display elements and **Inter** for dense transactional tables, data forms, and legal governance logs.

### Application Rules
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Reserved strictly for page-level titles, asset value readouts, sectional headings, and modal titles. Ensures authoritative character without decorative distractions.
- **Inter:** Deployed across all operational UI, metric graphs, data lists, form fields, and labels. Utilize `tabular-nums` for all asset values, account counts, monetary sums, and cryptographic key hashes.
- **Micro-labels (`label-sm`):** Render in uppercase with strict `0.05em` letter tracking when designating asset health, priority tags, security clearance tiers, and verification levels.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored on a rigid 8pt spatial cadence (with 4pt micro-steps for compact data displays). It uses a max-width container capped at `80rem` (1280px) to maintain readability across executive wide-screen monitors.

### Breakpoints & Grid Structure
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` page margins and `1rem` gutters. Multi-column metric summaries collapse into single-column vertical cards. Sticky contextual bottom sheets replace side slide-outs.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Primary sidebar collapses to an icon-rail navigation mode.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout with `2rem` page margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Persistent left-side institutional navigation (`16rem` fixed width) alongside a flexible, multi-column modular workspace.

### Spacing Strategy
- Form inputs, nested key-value pairs, and table line heights remain compact (`space-sm` to `space-base`) to preserve high density and contextual cohesion.
- Structural transitions between independent asset classes, security logs, and beneficiaries use generous air (`space-2xl`) to delineate distinct governance domains.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **low-contrast architectural outlines** paired with **subtle ambient shadows**, avoiding heavy drops or flashy floating effects.

### Surface Hierarchy
1. **Canvas (`#F8FAFC`):** The foundational substrate. Completely flat and non-reactive.
2. **Base Panel (`#FFFFFF`):** Primary cards, list enclosures, and ledger tables. Framed with a 1px solid border in `#E2E8F0` and an imperceptible diffused shadow: `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
3. **Elevated Elements (Dropdowns, Popovers, Action Menus):** Pure white background bounded by `#E2E8F0` with a calibrated resting shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
4. **Modals & Critical Overlays:** Floating structural panels bounded by `#CBD5E1` with high-aperture depth: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`, framed against a backdrop blur overlay (`rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.4)` with 4px backdrop blur).
5. **Interactive Vault Layers (Dark Tiers):** Used for highly classified or secure key management cards. Surface of `#0B132B` bordered by `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` with internal light-fall glows (`inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`).

## Shapes

The design system employs **Soft (`1`)** roundedness. Curvature is intentionally understated to reinforce structural integrity, precision, and financial permanence.

### Radii Tokens
- **Micro (2px / `0.125rem`):** Internal checkmarks, progress fill meters, micro-badges, and ledger indicators.
- **Base (4px / `0.25rem`):** Default for standard buttons, input fields, dropdown triggers, and interactive table rows.
- **Medium (8px / `0.5rem`):** Primary asset cards, dashboard widgets, modal containers, and perimeter trays.
- **Pill (9999px):** Strictly constrained to situational status tags, verification pills, and user profile monograms. Actionable buttons must never be full-pill shaped.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#0B132B`, text `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#0B132B`. Hover shifts to `#1C2541`. Height: 40px (Desktop), 44px (Mobile). Sharp typography (`title-sm`), rounded-base (`4px`). Focus ring: 2px offset with 2px ring in `#0D9488`.
- **Secondary:** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0F172A`, 1px border `#E2E8F0`. Hover shifts background to `#F8FAFC` and border to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Critical / Urgency:** Background `#D97706`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `#D97706`. Hover shifts to `#B45309`. Used only for irreversible succession actions, emergency triggers, or lockouts.

### Micro-Badges & Status Chips
- Height: 22px, padding: 0 8px, border-radius: 9999px (Pill), typography: `label-sm`.
- **Verified / Prepared (Teal):** Background `#F0FDFA`, text `#0F766E`, border `1px solid #CCFBF1`. Accompanied by a 6px solid `#0D9488` dot.
- **Urgent / Incomplete (Amber):** Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#B45309`, border `1px solid #FEF3C7`. Accompanied by a 6px pulsating `#D97706` dot.
- **Archived / Encrypted (Slate):** Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#475569`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height: 40px. Surface `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, padding 8px 12px, font `body-md`. Focus: border shifts to `#0D9488` with a 1px soft teal halo (`0 0 0 1px #0D9488`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 18x18px square (checkbox: 3px radius, radio: circular). Default border 1.5px solid `#CBD5E1`. Checked state: background `#0B132B`, check icon white.

### Structured Asset Cards
- White surface (`#FFFFFF`), 1px solid `#E2E8F0` border, 8px radius.
- Header contains asset type monogram, clear title (`headline-sm`), and a floating verification badge.
- Body displays key-value pairs formatted in tabular layout with `text-secondary` labels and `title-sm` values.
- Footer features a hairline `#F1F5F9` top rule, displaying sync timestamp and designated successor contact initials.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Succession Trigger Bar:** High-security horizontal component indicating transition protocol status. Deep navy background (`#0B132B`), white label, with a dual-key visual status tracker.
- **Audit Ledger Row:** Monospaced cryptographic key preview, precise timestamp, user agent tag, and state chip arranged in strict tabular layout with hover highlighting.
